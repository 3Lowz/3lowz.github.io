Surface container with a faint top-light edge. Use `glow`/`interactive` for featured experience cards, `glass` over imagery.

```jsx
<Card>Default surface</Card>
<Card variant="glow" interactive>Featured role</Card>
<Card variant="glass" padding="lg">Overlay panel</Card>
```

Variants: `solid | glass | glow`. `interactive` adds hover lift + teal halo. `padding`: `none | sm | md | lg`.
