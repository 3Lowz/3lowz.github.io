Monochrome portrait frame with optional teal ring/glow and presence dot. The brand portrait is cool B&W by default.

```jsx
<Avatar src="assets/portrait.jpg" alt="3Lowz" size="lg" status="online" />
<Avatar src="assets/portrait.jpg" size={48} ring={false} mono={false} />
```

`size`: `sm | md | lg | xl` or a px number. `ring`, `mono` toggle the accent frame and grayscale.
