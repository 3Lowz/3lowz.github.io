Icon-only button for social links and compact toolbar actions. Always pass `label` for accessibility.

```jsx
<IconButton icon={<i className="bi bi-github" />} label="GitHub" href="https://github.com/3Lowz" />
<IconButton icon={<i className="bi bi-linkedin" />} label="LinkedIn" variant="outline" shape="circle" />
```

Variants: `ghost | outline | solid`. Shapes: `rounded | circle`. Sizes: `sm | md | lg`.
